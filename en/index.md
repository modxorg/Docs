MODX Revolution (Revo) is an easy-to-use Content Management System (CMS) and Application Framework rolled into one. Despite MODX's limitless possibilities, you'll find it refreshingly intuitive to work with. At every step, MODX aims to give you Creative Freedom.

With MODX3, the platform has been modernised: the Manager got a new skin and layout tweaks, and the Setup and login screens were redesigned. [Learn more about upgrading to MODX 3](getting-started/upgrading-to-3.0).

In addition to this documentation site, the [MODX Community](https://community.modx.com) is vibrant and always willing to help. Go ahead and ask questions there — you'll be pleasantly surprised by how responsive MODX users are.

Experienced [MODX Professionals](https://modx.com/professionals) and [vendor-backed commercial support](https://modx.com/support/) options are also available, so whatever you need, you're covered.

### Extras

MODX Extras extend MODX's core functionality. Other CMSs refer to them as "Plugins" or "Modules". Many of the Extras are developed by the MODX core team, while others are by third parties. Although this is by no means an exhaustive reference, a large number of popular Extras are documented here.
