---
title: "package_installer_at_top"
description: "Pin the Installer entry to the top of the Extras menu."
---

**Name**: Pin Package-Installer at top
**Type**: Yes/No
**Default**: Yes

When enabled, the Installer is pinned to the top of the Extras menu in the manager. When disabled, it is positioned according to its `menuindex` like any other menu entry.

The value is read in `core/src/Revolution/modMenu.php`.
