## What the Rule Does

Hides a region of the form of the manager. A region is a whole tab of the form or a part of one; the list of the regions is on [Customizing Tabs](building-sites/client-proofing/form-customization/tabs "Customizing Tabs").

## Where It Is Set

The **Regions** tab of the set, in the **Visible** column. Clearing the checkbox of a region creates the rule; ticking it back removes the rule.
