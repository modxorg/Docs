## What the Rule Does

Replaces the label of a field of the form with a text of your own. The field itself keeps its name and its value; only the text next to it changes.

## Where It Is Set

The **Fields** tab of the set, in the **Label** column. A field with a label of its own gets the rule; an empty cell keeps the standard label of the field.
