## What the Rule Does

Hides a TV of the form of the manager. The TV is not merely hidden: its type becomes `hidden`, so it is not drawn and cannot be filled in.

## Where It Is Set

The **Template Variables** tab of the set, in the **Visible** column. Clearing the checkbox of a TV creates the rule; ticking it back removes the rule.
