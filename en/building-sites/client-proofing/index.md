The manager is a very powerful administrative interface, and when you've built a site for a client it is likely you'll want to tone a few things down a bit.

## The Look of the Manager

- [Custom Manager Themes](building-sites/client-proofing/custom-manager-themes) — override the files of the manager with a theme of your own.
- [Customizing the Login](building-sites/client-proofing/manager-login) — the logo, the background, the help block and the language of the login page.
- The setting `manager_logo` puts your own logo into the left panel of the manager.

## The Dashboards

[Dashboards](building-sites/client-proofing/dashboards) — the widgets on the page a user sees after logging in, and who sees which.

## The Forms of the Manager

[Form Customization](building-sites/client-proofing/form-customization) — the profiles that hide fields, rename tabs and change values, and the sets of rules they hold.

## The Access to the Manager

[Security](building-sites/client-proofing/security) — the policies, the permissions, the user groups, the roles and the resource groups that decide what a user may do.
