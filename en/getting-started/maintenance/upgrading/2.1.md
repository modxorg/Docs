Test the upgrade on a copy of your site before you touch production: uploading the upgrade overwrites all core files, and the only rollback is your backed-up files and database.

Version 2.1 removes the [legacy code](getting-started/maintenance/upgrading/evolution/legacy-removed-2.1) deprecated before 2.0, which is partly why the core got faster.

Most popular Extras are already upgraded and tested with 2.1, but some still use the old procedural API instead of the object-oriented one and may not work at all. Check your site in a safe environment before deploying.
